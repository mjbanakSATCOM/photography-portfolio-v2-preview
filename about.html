Welcome to Lantern & Leaves

Why "Lantern & Leaves"? When I thought about what really drives my photography, two images came to mind.

The first is a lantern. It all begins with God and the way He keeps leading me in life. His light goes before me, like a lantern on a dark path.

The second is leaves: nature, landscapes, and above all forests. Trees have always fascinated me, and there's nothing quite like getting lost in a forest when the light is just right. It's simply magical.

C.S. Lewis put it beautifully in his 1952 essay On Three Ways of Writing for Children:

"He does not despise real woods because he has read of enchanted woods: the reading makes all real woods a little enchanted."

— C. S. Lewis, On Three Ways of Writing for Children (1952)

Fairy tales don't pull us away from the real world. They add a layer of wonder to it. I couldn't agree more, and I think Tolkien, who loved trees as much as anyone, would agree too.

That's why, after reading The Hobbit or The Lord of the Rings, every walk in the forest or the woods becomes a stroll through Fangorn, Lothlórien, or even Mirkwood. You can almost picture Elves and other creatures hidden among the trees, watching. Our imaginations are hungry, and we can feed them good, nourishing food, or rubbish that starves and distorts them. In its own small way, photography tells those stories.

That's why, on the home page, you'll see verses from the Bible about God's light rotating alongside quotes that shine a light on nature and photography.

A little about me

I'm Mike, and I've loved photography for most of my life. As a kid I dabbled without much of a clue, a small spark that hadn't caught yet. That changed when my family and I moved to England. The history, the natural beauty, and long walks through the countryside inspired me to try to capture what I saw.

Along the way, an older local man who had been photographing for more than 40 years took me under his wing. He taught me more than any classroom could have. He worked mainly in black and white, and I fell in love with it right away. It's still at the heart of what I do. Over time I came to see how wonderful color can be too, because that's how God paints things.

I started out with Canon, which I loved, and later moved to Nikon with the mirrorless, full-frame Zf. I just love its retro design. When you spend as much time holding a camera as I do, the look and feel matter!

The photographs

Some of these pictures came from being in the right place at the right time. Others were planned down to the tiniest detail. All of them were made with care and passion. My greatest joy still comes from being out in God's creation, standing alone before an ocean or a forest, simply amazed at what He designed, as if it had been painted by a heavenly hand that very morning.

I hope you enjoy wandering through these images. If one stirs something in you, I'd love to hear about it. We all see photos, and what they hold, through our own lens.

God bless,
Mike
